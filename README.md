# Basketball Shootout

A low-poly basketball shootout built with three.js (`WebGPURenderer`). Hit the green zone of the shot meter
to score, move around the arc with every make, and set the ball on fire with a streak. 60 seconds, one high
score. Play with your webcam (shoot with your arms), keyboard, or a controller.
